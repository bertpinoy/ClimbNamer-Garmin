ClimbNamer

FOR GARMIN CONNECT

ClimbNamer finds the climbs on your Garmin Connect courses and names them with OpenStreetMap: a named summit or pass near the top of the climb, otherwise the road the climb follows. Rename a climb with ✎ in the panel on the course page; your own names are listed below.

Cache

Looked-up names, and the named summits of areas you rode or planned in, are stored in your browser so each climb and area is only looked up once. Clear the cache to look them up again, for example after OpenStreetMap was improved.
